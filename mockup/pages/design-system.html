Paragraph & Muted Hierarchy

Primary Paragraph Text

SPMIS provides a unified academic progress management portal with warm surfaces and clear hierarchy. Primary text is styled with var(--foreground) to maintain optimal readability and comfortable contrast.

Muted Descriptive Text

Secondary details, audit timestamps, and status explanations utilize var(--muted-foreground) to establish quiet visual depth without distracting from critical operational data.

Buttons & Actions

Interactive button primitives conforming to the .ui-btn specification
Standard .ui-btn Variants

Primary uses terracotta var(--primary). Secondary and outline utilize var(--secondary) and var(--border).

Button States & Icons

Disabled state enforces reduced opacity and cursor: not-allowed. Focus rings use var(--ring) with 2px offset.

Faculty Portal Page Actions

Standard action buttons used across the faculty portal headers and action sections. Utilizes base .ui-btn for primary page actions, and .ui-btn-secondary for grouped tertiary actions.

Workspace Footer

Fixed footer bar for admin and faculty portals — pinned to the bottom of the viewport
↑ Page content sits above the footer
© 2026 Faculty Portal. All rights reserved. System Version 2.4.0

Usage

<main>
  <div style="flex:1;min-height:0;overflow-y:auto;">
    <!-- Page content -->
  </div>
  <footer class="workspace-footer">
    <span>© 2026 Portal. All rights reserved.</span>
    <span>System Version 2.4.0</span>
  </footer>
</main>

The footer uses flex: none; height: auto; to override the workspace wildcard selector. Content is wrapped in a scrollable div with flex:1 so the footer stays pinned at the bottom.
