### I. CALL TO ORDER
The second project meeting was conducted from 9:00 PM to 10:10 PM. Nine members were present, two members were absent.

### II. AGENDA AND DISCUSSION

#### 1. Site Map and Web App Overview
The group reviewed the site map and discussed the general functionalities of the web application. Further technical specifications and details are documented in the project's designated markdown file.

#### 2. Role Division and Mockup Tasks
Development tasks for the mockup phase were categorized into two primary user roles: Faculty (Including Public/Auth) and Admin.

#### 3. Frontend Team Assignments
Development of the Public/Auth and Faculty routes was assigned to the Frontend team to leverage their larger team size.

#### 4. Backend Team Assignments
Development of the Admin routes was assigned to the Backend team, operating with direct support and oversight from the Project Manager.

#### 5. Quality Assurance (QA) Designations
QA responsibilities were distributed among the four dedicated members:
* **Public/Faculty Routes:** Assigned to three QA members.
* **Admin Routes:** Assigned to Reese Bantaculo, who is also serving as the QA Lead.

#### 6. Figma Mockup and Workflow Standardization
* Figma was selected for mockup visualization to streamline the transition to HTML.
* The finalized HTML output will be committed to the main project repository.
* The approved development workflow is as follows: Finalize Figma Mockup → Initial QA Review → HTML Conversion and Repository Push → Final QA Review.

#### 7. Goals for the Next Meeting
Deliverables expected by the next meeting include finalized Figma designs, completion of the initial QA phase, and commencement of HTML conversion.

#### 8. UI Theme Colors and Inspiration
* The Project Manager shared resources from tweakcn.com to inspire the overall UI theme and facilitate later CSS integration.
* Members proposed theme options and conducted a vote to determine the primary color palette. The 'Coffee' palette secured the majority with 5 votes, surpassing 'Blue' (3 votes).
* **Decision:** Coffee palette will be the main theme.
* A secondary poll was conducted via the Messenger group chat to select a specific 'Coffee' variant from nine proposed options. Voting concluded at 10:00 PM.
* **Poll Results:** Claude+ (6 votes), Portfolio (3 votes), Dark Forge (2 votes). All other options received zero votes.
* **Decision:** The 'Claude+' theme was officially adopted.
* Reference: https://tweakcn.com/themes/cmdght103000n04lh3e2ae93r

#### 9. Figma Workspace Access
Frontend Lead Carl Roy Arañez invited members to the SPMIS Mockup workspace on Figma. Members must provide their official VSU email addresses associated with their Figma accounts to utilize educational access benefits.

#### 10. Internal Task Delegation and Channel Usage
* The Frontend and Backend teams will autonomously delegate specific tasks among their respective members.
* Dedicated Discord channel categories (Frontend, Backend, and QA) have been established.
* Members were instructed to transition to their respective channels to finalize internal labor distribution.

### III. DECISION MADE
The following decisions were established during the meeting:
1. Development tasks for the system mockup are officially divided between the Frontend (Public/Faculty routes) and Backend (Admin routes) teams.
2. The 'Claude+' theme from tweakcn.com is adopted as the official UI design standard.
3. The development workflow is standardized as: Figma Design → Initial QA → HTML Conversion/Push → Final Transition QA.
4. Internal task delegation will be managed autonomously by the respective Frontend, Backend, and QA sub-teams within their dedicated channels.
