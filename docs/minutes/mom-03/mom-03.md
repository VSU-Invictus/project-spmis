### II. AGENDA AND DISCUSSION

#### 1. UI Theme and General Concerns
* The Backend group and Frontend group has finalized all assignments with some clarifications raised.
* Minor concerns regarding the bright orange theme were raised and supported by QA.
* Modals will shift to a darker aesthetic, while the modal borders will be changed to orange.
* NavBar colors will be revised to address brightness concerns.
* The Admin portal "Accept" and "Reject" buttons are causing font visibility issues; colors will be shifted to a warmer palette.
* Admin portal lists will be lengthened to reduce negative space.
* The current screen ratio is suboptimal and does not appear properly in full screen; this will be adjusted.

* Conditional rendering to hide unnecessary buttons - Ongoing.
* Pagination for the Faculty portal programs page - Ongoing.
